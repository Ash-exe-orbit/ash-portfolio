A community-first movement platform designed to bring people together through walking, jogging and running. Built around the belief that every pace belongs.

About Zuvori

Zuvori is an evolving movement and fitness platform that makes everyday activity more engaging through technology, personalisation and community.

The current build features GPS-powered activity tracking, distance and pace calculation, run history, activity streaks and customisable characters.

Nearby runner discovery and additional community features are still in development.
